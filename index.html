How to play

Goal

Flip two cards at a time and find the matching coin pairs. Clear the whole board in as few moves and as little time as you can.

Scoring

Each pair is worth 100. Matching pairs back to back builds a combo that adds 25 for every extra match in the row. A miss costs 10 points and breaks the combo. When the board is cleared you get a time bonus for finishing quickly.

Peek

Once per board you can use Peek to show every unmatched card for a moment. It costs 50 points.

Board sizes

Easy has 6 pairs, Normal 8, Hard 10 and Expert 12. Nano (XNO) is always one of the pairs. The other coins are picked at random. Your best score and time are saved for each size on this device.

Daily

The daily board is the same for everyone and changes at 00:00 UTC. Clear it to keep your daily streak. You can replay it as often as you like.

Coin facts

When you match a pair you see a short fact about that coin. The coins are drawn as simple tickers in their usual colours, not as official logos.

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra card backs. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
